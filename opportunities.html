Opportunity protection

Identifying details may be disclosed in stages

For selected opportunities, NordBridge may first share a non-identifying summary. Buyer names, tender references, links or documents can be disclosed later after appropriate written commercial protections are in place.

01

Initial fit

We assess whether the company has a credible capability match.

02

Summary

A high-level opportunity description can be provided without sensitive identifiers.

03

Commercial terms

Applicable fee, confidentiality and non-circumvention terms can be documented.

04

Disclosure

Relevant opportunity details can then be released for proper review and action.

Commercial models

Different opportunities require different structures.

Depending on the transaction, cooperation may use a success-based introduction fee, a separate paid tender-support scope, or a written arrangement for long-term orders and staged payments.

✓Success fee structures defined in writing.
✓Tender support can be scoped separately.
✓Long-term and repeat orders can use separate payment mechanics.
✓Direct contracting remains between principal parties.
