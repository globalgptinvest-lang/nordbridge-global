Process

What happens after you submit?

01

Initial fit review

NordBridge reviews the company, purpose, requested amount and basic financing profile.

02

Documents & compliance

If the case appears viable, we request the corporate, financial and KYC documents needed for a financing-readiness review.

03

Mandate & advisory fee

The scope, upfront advisory fee and success-fee terms are confirmed in writing before substantive work begins.

04

Funding-provider identification

NordBridge researches suitable independent third-party funding providers and coordinates introductions where appropriate. Final approval remains entirely with the lender or investor.
