Free Tender Checklist

Tender compliance checklist before bid submission

Use this practical checklist for RFP, RFQ, ITB, EOI and other procurement submissions. It is designed to help contractors and suppliers catch avoidable document and compliance gaps before the deadline.

Get a Free Tender Fit CheckProfessional Compliance Review
1. Eligibility

Confirm that the bidder can qualify

Legal eligibility

Check registration, licenses, permits, sanctions/exclusion requirements and any country restrictions stated in the procurement documents.

Experience & references

Confirm required years of experience, similar contracts, reference letters and evidence of completed work.

Financial criteria

Check turnover, financial statements, bank references, credit requirements or other financial thresholds.

2. Mandatory documents

Build a complete document list

Mark every mandatory form, declaration, certificate, attachment and supporting document. Note whether originals, notarization, translation, legalization or specific templates are required.

✓All required forms are present and use the correct version.
✓Names, company numbers and addresses are consistent across documents.
✓Certificates and licenses remain valid through the required period.
✓Required signatures, initials, stamps and powers of attorney are included.
3. Bid security & validity

Check commercial submission conditions

Bid bond / security

Confirm the amount, currency, wording, issuing bank or insurer, validity period and delivery format if a guarantee is required.

Offer validity

Verify the minimum validity period and make sure pricing, letters and guarantees use compatible dates.

Pricing forms

Check currencies, taxes, unit prices, totals, arithmetic and whether alternative pricing is permitted.

4. Technical compliance

Map the response to the buyer's requirements

Use a requirements matrix where possible. Each mandatory specification should point to the exact section, drawing, datasheet, certificate or narrative that demonstrates compliance.

5. Addenda & clarifications

Confirm that nothing changed after the original notice

Review all amendments, corrigenda, buyer Q&A and clarification notices. Make sure revised deadlines, quantities, specifications and forms are reflected in the final submission.

6. Final submission check

Check the package exactly as the buyer will receive it

✓Correct portal, email address or physical delivery location.
✓Correct deadline and local time zone.
✓Correct file names, formats, size limits and folder structure.
✓Technical and financial files separated where required.
✓Final package opened and checked after export/compression.
✓Submission receipt or portal confirmation saved.
