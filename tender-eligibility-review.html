Tender Eligibility Review

Check whether the tender fits your company before preparing the full bid.

NordBridge reviews the published qualification and eligibility criteria against the information provided by your company, helping identify obvious gaps before significant time and resources are committed.

Early-stage review

Understand the qualification barriers first

Eligibility requirements vary by procurement. A review can focus attention on the criteria that may determine whether a bid can proceed.

Company eligibility

Review stated registration, legal-status, geographic and bidder-type requirements.

Experience & references

Identify required project history, similar contracts, references and minimum experience periods.

Financial criteria

Map published turnover, financial-capacity, insurance, guarantee or other stated financial requirements.

Licenses & certifications

Identify mandatory licenses, standards, authorizations, certifications or professional registrations.

Output

A practical eligibility gap review

NordBridge can organize the published criteria into a working checklist and compare them with the information supplied by the bidder. Items that appear satisfied, unclear or missing can be identified for follow-up.

This is an independent commercial review, not a legal opinion or confirmation by the contracting authority. The authority makes the final eligibility and award decisions.
