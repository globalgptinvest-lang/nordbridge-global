Our Approach

Clear steps. Protected introductions. Direct contracting.

NordBridge structures the pre-contract process so each party understands the next step, what information is being shared and which commercial terms apply.

How we work

A six-step commercial process

The exact sequence may vary by opportunity, but the core principle remains the same: assess the fit, document the commercial framework and move toward direct contracting.

01

Capability Review

We review company profile, scope, sector, geography and basic eligibility against the opportunity.

02

Protected Summary

When appropriate, a non-identifying opportunity summary is shared before sensitive details.

03

Written Terms

The parties can agree applicable fee, support scope, confidentiality and non-circumvention terms.

04

Controlled Disclosure

Relevant buyer, tender or project details are released after agreed protections are in place.

05

Document Coordination

NordBridge can coordinate clarifications, missing documents and submission readiness within the agreed scope.

06

Direct Contracting

The buyer and contractor or supplier contract directly. NordBridge remains in its agreed introducer or coordination role.

Protection & transparency

Commercial protection without overstating our role.

NordBridge does not claim to represent a contracting authority unless expressly authorized, does not guarantee awards and does not seek to bypass procurement rules.

Commercial protection is designed to preserve the value of an introduction while allowing the principal parties to perform their own legal, financial, technical and commercial due diligence.

✓Written fee and payment triggers where applicable.
✓Confidentiality and non-circumvention where appropriate.
✓Document review before submission where agreed.
✓Direct contracts remain between the principal parties.
