International Procurement Support

UN tender support and bid-document preparation.

NordBridge provides independent commercial support to contractors and suppliers pursuing procurement opportunities published by UN organizations and other international institutions. We help organize requirements, eligibility, compliance and submission readiness.

Tender support

From procurement notice to a submission-ready document set

Support is tailored to the specific procurement and may include review of RFQ, RFP, ITB, EOI and prequalification documentation. The bidder remains responsible for its representations, pricing, technical solution and final submission.

Organizations & procurement types

Support for international institutional procurement

Companies may approach NordBridge when evaluating opportunities published by organizations such as UNICEF, UNOPS, IOM and other international buyers. NordBridge is an independent service provider and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of these organizations.

Where permitted, support can be provided under a separately agreed fixed professional fee. Any success fee or introduction arrangement is documented separately in writing where applicable.
