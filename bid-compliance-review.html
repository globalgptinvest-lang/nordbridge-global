Bid Compliance Review

Check the tender requirements before you submit.

NordBridge provides an independent review of tender requirements, eligibility, mandatory documents and submission readiness for contractors and suppliers preparing competitive bids.

Pre-submission review

Identify avoidable compliance gaps

A bid can be commercially strong and still face rejection if a mandatory form, declaration, certificate, signature, format or eligibility requirement is missed. The review is designed to surface those gaps before submission.

Mandatory requirements

Map required forms, declarations, certificates, references, signatures, formats and submission instructions.

Eligibility check

Review stated qualification criteria and flag information that appears missing, unclear or unsupported.

Technical & commercial consistency

Check the document set for obvious gaps or inconsistencies against the published procurement requirements.

Final readiness checklist

Provide a practical list of outstanding items for the bidder to resolve before final submission.

Scope

Independent review, not an award guarantee

NordBridge can review the procurement package and the documents provided by the bidder. The bidder remains responsible for factual accuracy, pricing, technical commitments, legal representations and the final submission.

The scope and professional fee are agreed before work begins. Where appropriate, broader tender-document support can be added separately.
