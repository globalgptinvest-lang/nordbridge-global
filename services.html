Tender Support

Fixed-fee tender support and bid-document review

If your company has already identified a tender, NordBridge can support the preparation process for an agreed upfront professional fee. Support may include requirements analysis, eligibility and compliance review, document checklist, review and organization of technical and commercial documents, identification of missing information and a final compliance review before submission.

Analysis

Tender Requirements Review

We review the procurement package and identify key requirements, deadlines, eligibility criteria and submission obligations.

Documents

Bid Document Support

We help organize the required documentation, identify missing items and review the package for completeness.

Commercial model

Fixed Fee · Separate Success Fee Where Applicable

Tender support is provided for an agreed upfront fixed fee. A success fee applies only where separately agreed in writing for a relevant sourced or protected opportunity. NordBridge does not guarantee an award.
