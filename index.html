What we do

From opportunity discovery to a protected business connection.

NordBridge works on the commercial coordination layer between buyers, contractors and suppliers. The contracting parties remain responsible for their own technical, legal and commercial commitments.

01

Opportunity & Tender Sourcing

We identify procurement and commercial opportunities that match a company's real capabilities, geography and delivery profile.

02

Contractor & Supplier Matching

We connect relevant buyers and opportunities with companies capable of supplying the required products, services or project execution.

03

Tender Document Support

Review of requirements, eligibility, missing documents, deadlines and submission readiness. Submission support is provided only where permitted and authorized in writing.

04

Pre-Contract Coordination

Structured communication, clarifications, document exchange and commercial follow-up through the pre-contract stage.

05

Supplier & Subcontractor Sourcing

Identification of relevant suppliers, specialist subcontractors and supporting partners where a tender or project requires them.

06

International Business Development

Independent cross-border introductions for companies seeking new markets, procurement channels and commercial relationships.

How we work

A clear, protected process.

For selected opportunities, identifying details are disclosed only after the relevant commercial and confidentiality protections are in place.

Capability Review

We review the contractor or supplier profile against the opportunity's scope and eligibility requirements.

Protected Opportunity Summary

Where required, an initial non-identifying description is shared before buyer, tender reference, links or documents are disclosed.

Written Commercial Terms

The parties agree the applicable Introducer / Success Fee, support scope, confidentiality and non-circumvention terms in writing.

Controlled Disclosure

After protection is agreed, the available opportunity details and documents can be shared for review.

Tender & Document Coordination

NordBridge may support compliance checks, missing-document control, clarifications and submission preparation where agreed.

Direct Contracting

The buyer and contractor contract directly. NordBridge's remuneration follows the written agreement and agreed payment triggers.

◇
Commercial protection before sensitive disclosure

Buyer names, tender references, links, solicitation documents and other identifying details may be withheld until appropriate written protection is in place. This protects the introduced opportunity without interfering with applicable procurement rules.

Commercial structures

Flexible terms for different transaction types.

Commercial terms are agreed case by case and documented before protected information is disclosed.

Model 01

Success-Based Introduction

An introduction or transaction facilitation fee linked to a successful contract, order or agreed commercial milestone.

  • Percentage agreed in writing
  • Defined payment triggers
  • Non-circumvention protection
Model 02

Tender Support

Separate paid support for document preparation, review and bid coordination, with success-based terms where separately agreed.

  • Scope defined before work starts
  • Document and deadline control
  • Authorized submission support where permitted
Model 03

Long-Term Orders

Written fee mechanics for framework contracts, call-offs, staged payments, extensions, renewals and repeat orders arising from an introduction.

  • Payment schedule defined in agreement
  • Actual contract/order value tracked where applicable
  • Extensions and renewals can be covered
